## Hesitations and friction

The main slowdowns were not about player quality or fit data. They were about workflow friction.

- I wish I could see roster impact and team need without leaving the player card.
- I wish I could assign a winner, bid, and classification in one click instead of filling the small form field by field.
- I wish I could compare and decide without hunting across League, Teams, Players, and Draft Hub tabs.
- I wish I could see the draft board and my working-state notes in the same place while I was making the decision.
- I wish I could move faster through the available players list without re-searching the same names.
- I wish I could see team need and remaining draft pressure in-line with the player row.
- I wish I could batch update a hot list instead of entering each assignment one by one.
- I wish I could understand the draft impact of a pick from the player screen without opening a separate owner view.

## Reality check

The app is usable enough to run a draft tonight if the goal is a practical, local working-state draft board. The draft state is visible, assignments are tracked, and the app does not overwrite the source data. That said, the overall flow still feels like a high-functioning research tool with a good working-state layer, not a polished live-draft machine.

## Answers

### 1. Could I run my draft tonight?

Yes, with a caveat: I could run a draft tonight if I was comfortable using this as a guided decision-support and local working-state workflow rather than a fully optimized live-draft product. It is good enough to keep the draft moving, but not yet effortless enough to feel completely comfortable in a 30-minute live environment.

### 2. What slowed me down?

The biggest slowdown was not evaluating players. It was the friction between views:

- switching between League, Teams, Players, and Draft Hub to do basic research
- entering team, bid, and classification in the working-state form after already deciding
- not seeing team impact or roster pressure in-line with the player card
- repeated checks to confirm the player was still available before committing a working-state assignment
- no fast bulk-update path when the board was moving quickly

### 3. What is the SINGLE highest-value improvement remaining?

The single highest-value improvement is a true one-click draft assignment in the Draft Hub: assign winner, bid, classification, and status from the same player row without leaving the current decision view, with team-impact context visible inline.

That gives the app the fastest possible “decision -> document -> move on” loop, which is the difference between a workable rehearsal and a confident draft-night tool.
