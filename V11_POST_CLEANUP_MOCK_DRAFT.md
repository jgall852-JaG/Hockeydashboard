# V11 Post-Cleanup Mock Draft Review

## Bottom line

The cleanup improved the workflow. League, Teams, Players, and Draft Hub now feel more separated and faster to scan.

## CRITICAL

- None found.

## HIGH

- Draft Hub comparison still requires too much digging for league-context decisions. Ownership, cost, term, rights, and retention are available, but not visible fast enough on the main decision surfaces.
- The Draft Board is still compact in a way that hides decision-critical context. It is good for scanning names, value, and risk, but not yet ideal for auction-speed judgment.

## MEDIUM

- Teams and Players now hold most of the roster and player detail, but the handoff back to Draft Hub still feels manual when I want to move from a quick board read to a deeper explanation.
- The decision summary is useful, but when two players are close, I still want a sharper “why this one over that one” delta.
- League status is clear, but the fastest path from league overview to draft decision still depends on moving across multiple tabs.

## LOW

- Some labels are still generic for draft-day speed.
- Queue and scarcity are useful, but I still notice the extra step of selecting a player before the full decision context becomes clear.

## What worked better after cleanup

1. The tab split makes the app easier to think about.
2. League now works as a true starting point instead of a cluttered landing page.
3. Draft Hub is faster to scan because it focuses on Best Available, Board, Queue, Scarcity, and Decision Summary.

## What information was still hard to find

- Owner/asset context at a glance
- Cost and term on the fastest draft surfaces
- Rights and retention status without opening deeper detail
- Clear side-by-side comparison deltas

## Top 3 improvements that would most improve draft-day decision making

1. Surface compact ownership / cost / term / rights / retention badges directly in Draft Hub rows and comparison.
2. Add a stronger compare summary that explains the difference between the two players in one line.
3. Make deep context one click away from Draft Hub with clearer links into Players and Teams.

## Final answer

Yes, the current setup is good enough for a real mock draft, but the main hesitation still comes from hidden league context during fast decisions.
